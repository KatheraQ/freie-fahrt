Contracts, the contract of carriage and liability

Every taxi trip is a contract. On top of that come contracts around the business: buying vehicles, leasing, renting, loans. In this lesson you learn the main types of contract, how a contract of carriage is formed and who is liable when something happens: accidents, damaged luggage and forgotten items.

Exam area 1.6 · Civil law, contract of carriageapprox. 35 minutesBGB (§§ 104 ff., 145 ff., 195, 433, 488, 535, 581, 631, 823, 978)§§ 7, 8a, 12, 18 StVG§ 23 PBefG§ 11 BOKraftDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • tell the most important types of contract apart
  • explain how a contract of carriage is formed and what duties follow from it
  • distinguish keeper's liability and fault-based liability
  • know the liability for personal injury and property damage to passengers
  • handle lost property correctly

How is a contract formed?

A contract is formed by two matching declarations: an offer and an acceptance §§ 145 ff. BGB. It does not have to be in writing. Clear conduct is also enough (schlüssiges Verhalten).

Taxi example

A passenger gets in at the taxi rank and says: “To the main station, please.” The driver starts the meter and drives off. A contract of carriage (Beförderungsvertrag) has been formed, without any signature.

Legal capacity

  • Children under 7 have no legal capacity. They cannot make contracts § 104 BGB.
  • Minors aged 7 to 17 have limited capacity. Their contract is valid if the parents agree or if they pay immediately with their own pocket money §§ 106 ff., 110 BGB.

Limitation period

Claims usually become time-barred after 3 years. The period starts at the end of the year in which the claim arose §§ 195, 199 BGB. An unpaid invoice for a trip in May 2026 therefore becomes time-barred at the end of 31.12.2029.

Remember

The contract of carriage is a contract for work (Werkvertrag): the result is owed, i.e. safe arrival at the destination. The employment contract with the driver, by contrast, is a service contract.

Duties under the contract of carriage

The operator owesThe passenger owes

If a passenger refuses to pay, the driver can have his personal details taken and call the police. Anyone who takes a trip without intending to pay commits a criminal offence (fraud). Some tariffs allow an advance payment § 51 (1) sentence 2 No. 3 PBefG.

Who is liable in an accident?

In road traffic there are two kinds of liability:

Keeper's liability (strict liability) Halterhaftung, GefährdungshaftungThe vehicle keeper is liable for damage caused by operating his vehicle, even without fault. Reason: a car is dangerous in itself. Excluded only in case of force majeure (höhere Gewalt).§ 7 StVG
Driver's liabilityThe driver is liable if he caused the accident by fault. His fault is presumed. He must prove that he is not to blame.§ 18 StVG
Tort liability deliktische HaftungAnyone who intentionally or negligently injures another person's life, body, health or property is liable for damages without limit.§ 823 BGB

Maximum amounts of keeper's liability

Personal injury per accident5.000.000 €
Property damage per accident1.000.000 €
for paid passenger transport, if more than 8 passengers are injured: for each further passenger additionally600.000 €

§ 12 StVG

With fault under § 823 BGB there is no upper limit. For all this your vehicle needs motor liability insurance (lesson 2.5).

Passengers are specially protected

  • Personal injury: in paid, commercial passenger transport, keeper's liability towards passengers for death and injury may neither be excluded nor limited § 8a StVG. A sign saying “ride at your own risk” has no effect in a taxi.
  • Property damage (e.g. luggage, laptop): the operator can only exclude his liability towards each passenger for the part above 1.000 €. Not even that is possible in case of intent or gross negligence § 23 PBefG.
Example

Through a minor driving error, a passenger's suitcase is destroyed. Damage: 1.600 €. The operator has excluded liability above 1.000 € in his conditions of carriage. He must pay 1.000 €. If the driver had been grossly negligent (e.g. ran a red light), he would have to pay the full 1.600 €.

Lost property in the taxi

  • After every trip the driver must check whether passengers have left anything behind § 11 BOKraft.
  • If he cannot return it immediately, he hands it in without delay to the operator or the place named by the authority, for example the lost property office.
  • If a passenger finds something in the taxi, he must hand it to the driver or operator § 978 BGB.
  • Finder's reward: a passenger who finds something can receive a halved finder's reward from a value of 50 €. The driver as an employee of the company gets no finder's reward § 978 (2) BGB.

Typical mistakes

!“Without a written contract there is no contract of carriage.”Getting in, naming the destination and driving off is enough (conduct).
!“The contract of carriage is a service contract.”It is a contract for work: the result is owed.
!“With a sticker ‘no liability for personal injury’ I'm protected.”Towards passengers in paid transport this has no effect (§ 8a StVG).
!“The driver gets a finder's reward for the phone he found.”Employees have no claim; passengers get a halved reward from 50 €.
