Insurance

A single serious accident can ruin a small taxi business if insurance is missing or doesn't pay. Some insurance is mandatory, other insurance is very sensible. Just as important are your duties towards the insurer. If you break them, you may have to pay yourself.

Exam area 2.6 · Insuranceapprox. 25 minutes§§ 1, 4 PflVG and Annex§§ 5, 6 KfzPflVV§ 115 VVGSGB VIIDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • name the mandatory insurance in a taxi and Mietwagen business
  • know the minimum sums insured for motor liability
  • choose sensible voluntary insurance for the business and the owner
  • explain contractual duties (Obliegenheiten) and the consequences of breaking them

Mandatory insurance

Motor liability insurance Kfz-HaftpflichtversicherungMandatory for every vehicle. It pays for damage you cause to others with the vehicle, including your passengers. No registration without it.§ 1 PflVG
Statutory accident insuranceVia the Berufsgenossenschaft (BG Verkehr). For employees and for you as a taxi or Mietwagen operator (statutes). Covers accidents at work and on the way to work.SGB VII
Employees' social insuranceHealth, care, pension, unemployment insurance (lesson 1.8).
The operator's health insuranceEveryone must have health insurance, statutory or private.

Minimum sums insured for motor liability

Type of damage (per accident)At least
Personal injury7.500.000 €
Property damage1.300.000 €
Pure financial loss50.000 €

Annex to § 4 (2) PflVG

For vehicles with more than 9 seats (excluding the driver's seat) the sums increase per additional seat. For normal taxis and Mietwagen the basic amounts apply. In practice most operators agree much higher sums, often a flat 100 million euros.

Direct claim

An injured party can turn directly to your motor liability insurer § 115 VVG.

State the correct use

The vehicle must be insured as a taxi or Mietwagen. The premiums are higher than for private cars. Anyone who insures a vehicle as a private car and carries passengers commercially breaks a contractual duty (use clause). The insurer still pays the other party, but can reclaim money from you.

InsuranceWhat it paysFor taxi/Mietwagen

And for you privately as the owner

  • Health insurance with daily sickness benefit: as a self-employed person you get no sick pay.
  • Occupational disability insurance: if you can no longer drive, your income is gone.
  • Retirement provision: voluntary pension insurance or private provision.
  • Long-term care insurance: mandatory, part of health insurance.

Your duties towards the insurer

Contractual duties (Obliegenheiten) are rules of conduct from the insurance contract. Breaking them risks the insurer paying nothing or only part, or reclaiming money (recourse).

Before the damage (examples)After the damage (examples)
Use the vehicle only for the agreed purpose (taxi/Mietwagen)Report the damage without delay
Don't let anyone drive without a licenceNo hit-and-run, stay at the scene
Don't let anyone drive under alcohol or drugsGive truthful and complete information
Don't allow unauthorised useKeep the damage as small as possible

§§ 5, 6 KfzPflVV

How much can motor liability insurance reclaim?

The liability insurer always pays the other party. But it can reclaim money from you. In motor liability this is limited:

Breach of a duty before the damage (e.g. drunk driver, wrong use)up to 5.000 € per person
Breach of a duty after the damage (intentional or grossly negligent)up to 2.500 €
particularly serious intentional breach of the duty to clarify after the damage (e.g. hit-and-run)up to 5.000 €

§ 5 (3), § 6 KfzPflVV

In comprehensive insurance these limits don't exist. There the insurer can reduce payment for gross negligence and refuse it entirely for intent.

Typical mistakes

!“Motor liability also pays for damage to my own taxi.”No. Own vehicle damage: comprehensive/partial cover (Kasko).
!“I drive Mietwagen with my private car insurance.”The vehicle must be insured for commercial passenger transport.
!“Minimum cover for personal injury: 5 m €.”5 m € is the liability limit under § 12 StVG. The minimum sum insured is 7,5 m €.
!“After a hit-and-run the liability insurer pays the victim nothing.”It pays the victim. It can reclaim up to 5.000 € from the insured.
