Employment law: from contract to dismissal

As soon as you hire a driver, you are an employer. Then many laws apply: minimum wage, written proof of working conditions, holidays, sick pay and protection against dismissal. Customs checks the taxi trade particularly often because it counts as a sector with a risk of undeclared work. Mistakes quickly cost a lot of money here.

Exam area 1.4 · Employment lawapprox. 40 minutesNachwG, MiLoG, SchwarzArbGBUrlG, EFZG, TzBfG§§ 622, 623, 626 BGB, KSchGAGG, MuSchG, ArbSchG, SGB IXDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • name the duties under an employment contract
  • know what must be in the written statement of working conditions and by when
  • apply minimum wage, time recording and the ID duty in the taxi trade
  • calculate holidays and sick pay
  • explain fixed-term contracts and dismissal with notice periods
  • know the most important protective laws for employees

The employment contract

An employment contract (Arbeitsvertrag) is concluded when employer and employee agree. It can even be made orally. Still, you must record the most important conditions in writing (see below).

Employee's dutiesEmployer's duties
Work, in personPay wages, at least the minimum wage
Follow instructions on content, place and time of work § 106 GewODuty of care: protect health and safety
Loyalty: keep business secrets, no competitionGrant holidays, pay social insurance, issue a reference § 109 GewO

Written statement of the essential working conditions

The Act on Proof of Working Conditions (Nachweisgesetz) requires you to write down the main conditions, sign them and give them to the employee. There are three deadlines § 2 NachwG:

At the latest …What?
on the 1st working daynames and addresses, pay (amount, supplements, due date, method of payment), working time, breaks, rest periods, and for shift work the shift system
on the 7th calendar daystart date, end date if fixed-term, place of work, job description, probation period, on-call work, overtime
after 1 monthholidays, training, company pension, dismissal procedure with notice periods and deadline for legal action, reference to collective agreements
Special rule in the taxi trade

In many sectors an email or PDF (text form) is now enough. This relief does not apply to the passenger transport trade. You must issue the statement on paper, sign it and hand it over § 2 (1) NachwG with § 2a SchwarzArbG. A written employment contract with all details replaces the statement.

Minimum wage, time recording, ID

FromMinimum wage per hour (gross)
1 January 202613,90 €
1 January 202714,60 €

§ 1 MiLoG, Fifth Minimum Wage Adjustment Regulation (MiLoV5)

The minimum wage (Mindestlohn) applies to every working hour. Waiting time at the taxi rank is also working time if the driver must be ready for customers there. Pure revenue-based pay (e.g. 40 % of revenue) is only allowed if at the end every hour is paid at least the minimum wage.

Example (sample values)
Driver works 160 hours in the month (incl. waiting time) Revenue share: 40 % of 5.200 € net revenue = 2.080,00 € Minimum wage: 160 h × 13,90 € = 2.224,00 € → You must pay at least 2.224,00 €, i.e. 144,00 € more.

Duties because of checks for undeclared work

The passenger transport trade is on the list of particularly checked sectors § 2a SchwarzArbG. The financial control unit of customs (Finanzkontrolle Schwarzarbeit) checks often. Therefore:

  • ID: every driver must carry an ID card, passport or substitute document while working and show it to customs on request § 2a SchwarzArbG.
  • Record working time: start, end and length of daily working time, at the latest by the 7th calendar day after the working day. Keep the records for at least 2 years § 17 (1) MiLoG.
  • Immediate notification to social insurance at the latest when work starts (lesson 1.8).

Mini-job

A mini-job (geringfügige Beschäftigung) exists if monthly earnings do not exceed the mini-job limit. The limit is linked to the minimum wage: in 2026 it is 603 € per month § 8 (1a) SGB IV. Minimum wage, holidays, sick pay and time recording also apply to mini-jobbers.

Holidays and sick pay

Holidays

  • Statutory minimum holiday: 24 working days (Werktage) per year. Working days are Monday to Saturday. With a 5-day week that is 20 days, i.e. 4 weeks § 3 BUrlG.
  • The full entitlement only arises after 6 months of employment (waiting period) § 4 BUrlG. Before that, 1/12 accrues for each full month.
  • Holidays must be taken in the current year. They can only be carried over for urgent reasons, then until 31 March of the next year § 7 (3) BUrlG.
Part-time holiday = 24 working days ÷ 6 × working days per week

Example: a driver works 3 days a week. 24 ÷ 6 × 3 = 12 days of holiday.

Sick pay

  • If an employee is ill, the employer continues to pay wages for up to 6 weeks. After that the health insurer pays sickness benefit § 3 (1) EFZG.
  • The entitlement only arises after 4 weeks of uninterrupted employment § 3 (3) EFZG.
  • If the illness lasts longer than 3 calendar days, a doctor's certificate must be available, i.e. on day 4. The employer may ask for it earlier. Today you usually retrieve the sick note electronically from the health insurer (eAU) § 5 EFZG.

Small businesses (usually up to 30 employees) take part in the U1 levy. The health insurer then refunds part of the sick pay (lesson 1.8).

Fixed-term and part-time contracts

An employment contract can be fixed-term § 14 TzBfG:

  • Without an objective reason: at most 2 years, with at most 3 extensions within that time. Not allowed if the employee has worked for you before.
  • With an objective reason: for example covering for a sick driver. Then also longer.
  • The fixed term must be agreed in writing and before work starts. Otherwise the contract is deemed open-ended §§ 14 (4), 16 TzBfG.

Part-time employees may not be treated worse than full-time employees just because they work part-time § 4 TzBfG.

Dismissal

A notice of dismissal (Kündigung) must be in writing with a handwritten signature. Dismissal by email, text message or WhatsApp is invalid § 623 BGB.

Notice periods

SituationNotice period
Probation period (max. 6 months)2 weeks, to any day
Basic period (for both sides)4 weeks to the 15th or the end of a month
Dismissal by the employer, employed for 2 years1 month to the end of the month
… for 5 / 8 / 10 years2 / 3 / 4 months to the end of the month
… for 12 / 15 / 20 years5 / 6 / 7 months to the end of the month

§ 622 BGB

The longer periods only apply if the employer gives notice. An employee can always give notice with the basic period of 4 weeks unless the contract says otherwise.

Summary dismissal

For an important reason, for example stealing from the till or driving under the influence of alcohol, dismissal without notice is possible. It must be given within 2 weeks of the employer learning of the reason § 626 BGB. Usually a written warning (Abmahnung) is needed first, except for very serious misconduct.

Protection Against Dismissal Act

The Protection Against Dismissal Act (Kündigungsschutzgesetz) applies if the business has more than 10 employees and the employment has lasted more than 6 months §§ 1, 23 KSchG. Then a dismissal needs a reason: in the person (e.g. driving licence withdrawn), in the conduct (e.g. repeated lateness after a warning) or operational (e.g. a taxi is taken off the road). Anyone who wants to fight it must file a claim at the labour court within 3 weeks § 4 KSchG.

Special protection against dismissal applies, among others, to pregnant women and mothers up to 4 months after birth § 17 MuSchG, severely disabled people (consent of the integration office required) § 168 SGB IX and works council members.

Typical mistakes

!“I dismiss the driver by WhatsApp.”Dismissal only in writing with a signature (§ 623 BGB).
!“I don't pay for waiting time at the taxi rank.”Waiting on standby is working time and counts for the minimum wage.
!“I send the employment contract as a PDF by email.”In passenger transport: on paper, signed, handed over.
!“Fixed-term without reason: 3 years.”At most 2 years, at most 3 extensions, in writing before the start.
!“Sick pay: 6 months.”6 weeks.
