Licence, access to the profession and inspections

Without a licence (Genehmigung) you may not carry a single passenger for money. In this lesson you learn which requirements you must meet, how much equity you need, how the application works and what happens if you break the rules. Exam questions on equity and deadlines are very common.

Exam area 1.1 · Passenger transport lawapprox. 30 minutes§§ 2, 13–17, 19, 25, 54, 61 PBefG§§ 1–9 PBZugVDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • name the four licence requirements under § 13 (1) PBefG
  • calculate the equity needed for taxi and Mietwagen
  • explain how reliability and professional competence are proven
  • describe the licensing procedure with its deadlines
  • know the duties around the licence document
  • understand inspections, fines and revocation of the licence

The four requirements

The authority may only grant the licence if all four requirements are met § 13 (1) sentence 1 PBefG:

1. Safety and financial capacity Sicherheit und LeistungsfähigkeitThe business is safe. You have enough money to run it properly (financial standing).No. 1
2. Reliability ZuverlässigkeitThere are no facts showing that you or your managers are unreliable.No. 2
3. Professional competence fachliche EignungYou, or the person managing the business, are professionally competent.No. 3
4. Office in Germany Betriebssitz im InlandYour registered office or branch is in Germany.No. 4

How these points are checked is set out in the Regulation on Access to the Profession (Berufszugangsverordnung für den Straßenpersonenverkehr, PBZugV).

Memory aid

Safe, Reliable, Competent, German office: “Smart Riders Can Go”. In German: Sicherheit, Zuverlässigkeit, fachliche Eignung, Inland.

Reliability

You are reliable if nothing suggests that you will run the business against the law or endanger the public. Signs of unreliability are in particular § 1 (1) PBZugV:

  • final convictions for serious crimes,
  • serious breaches of the PBefG and its regulations,
  • serious breaches of employment and social law, for example working and rest times,
  • serious breaches of traffic and vehicle safety rules (StVG, StVO, StVZO),
  • serious breaches of tax and levy duties,
  • driving without motor liability insurance (§ 6 Compulsory Insurance Act),
  • serious breaches of environmental rules.

As proof, the authority can ask for a certificate of good conduct (Führungszeugnis) and an extract from the central trade register, as well as certificates from the tax office and social insurance § 1 (3) PBZugV.

Financial standing: how much equity?

You must have enough money to start and run the business properly. For taxi and Mietwagen there is a fixed minimum: equity and reserves (Eigenkapital und Reserven) must be at least one quarter of the amounts EU law sets for bus companies § 2 (1) No. 2 PBZugV, Art. 7 Reg. (EC) 1071/2009.

VehicleBus (EU law)Taxi / Mietwagen (¼)
first vehicle9.000 €2.250 €
each additional vehicle5.000 €1.250 €
Example

You want to start with 3 Mietwagen.

1st vehicle = 2.250 € 2nd and 3rd vehicle = 2 × 1.250 € = 2.500 € Minimum equity = 4.750 €

In addition, your ability to pay must not be at risk. You must not have significant arrears in taxes or social insurance contributions § 2 (1) No. 1 PBZugV.

Reserves can also include, for example, unencumbered private assets of the liable operator or subordinated loans § 2 (3) PBZugV.

Professional competence: three ways

You are professionally competent if you have the knowledge listed in Annex 3 of the PBZugV § 3 (2) PBZugV. That is exactly the content of this course. You can prove it in three ways:

  1. The exam (Fachkundeprüfung) at the IHK where you live. This is the normal way. §§ 4, 5 PBZugV
  2. An equivalent final examination recognised by the state authority. § 6 PBZugV
  3. Management experience of at least 3 years in a German taxi or Mietwagen company. It must have ended no more than 2 years before the application. The IHK checks this and may hold an interview. § 7 PBZugV

The owner does not have to be competent personally. It is enough if a person appointed to manage the business is, for example an employed operations manager § 13 (1) No. 3 PBefG.

The procedure: from application to licence

  1. Apply to the licensing authority, usually the city or district administration, with proof of all four requirements and details of the vehicles.
  2. Consultation: the authority asks for opinions from the municipality of the office, the trade supervisory office, the IHK, trade unions and transport associations. They have 2 weeks. § 14 (2) and (4) PBefG
  3. Decision within 3 months. The authority can extend this once by up to 3 months. If it does not decide in time, the licence is deemed granted. § 15 (1) PBefG
  4. Licence document (Genehmigungsurkunde): you receive it once the decision is final. There is no licence “on trial” or subject to revocation. § 15 (2) and (4) PBefG
Extra for taxis

The authority checks whether new taxis would endanger the functioning of the local taxi trade. It looks at demand, taxi density, income and cost situation and business closures. For this it can set an observation period of at most 1 year. That is why many cities have waiting lists. § 13 (4) PBefG

Applications are processed in order of receipt, separately for new applicants and existing operators. Anyone who does not intend to run the taxi business as their main occupation is placed further back. New applicants get their first licence for only 2 years and may not transfer it during that time. § 13 (5) PBefG

The licence document

The licence document states, among other things § 17 (1) PBefG:

  • name, residence and registered office of the operator,
  • the type of transport (taxi or Mietwagen),
  • the period of validity,
  • conditions and requirements,
  • the registration numbers of the vehicles used.
Everyday duties
  • Carry it: every vehicle must carry the licence or a shortened official copy (gekürzte amtliche Ausfertigung), on paper or electronically. Show it on request. § 17 (4) PBefG
  • Vehicle change: if you replace a vehicle or take one out of service, submit the document to the authority for amendment. § 17 (2) PBefG

Changes, transfer, death

You also need a licence for § 2 (2) PBefG:

  • an expansion or significant change of the business (for example more vehicles),
  • the transfer of the licence to someone else,
  • handing over the management of operations to someone else.

Taxi licences may only be transferred together with the whole business or an independent part of it. Pure trading in “licences” is not allowed § 2 (3) PBefG.

If the operator dies or becomes unable to act

  • Death: the heirs may continue the business provisionally. They must apply for their own licence within 3 months after the period for disclaiming the inheritance ends. § 19 (1) and (2) PBefG
  • Legal incapacity: a third party may continue the business for up to 1 year, in justified cases 6 months longer. § 19 (4) PBefG

Inspections, fines and revocation

The licensing authority checks whether you comply with the PBefG, its regulations and the licence conditions § 54 (1) PBefG. You must report significant changes in your business to it promptly and on your own initiative § 54 (2) PBefG. You must give information, show your books and allow inspections § 54a PBefG. At least every 5 years the authority checks whether you still meet the requirements § 9 PBZugV.

§ 61 PBefG

Revocation of the licence

  • The authority must revoke the licence if a requirement of § 13 (1) is no longer met, for example reliability. You are unreliable in particular if you ignore traffic safety despite a written warning or repeatedly break the PBefG. § 25 (1) PBefG
  • The authority may revoke it if financial standing is lacking or you repeatedly break your duties under employment, social or tax law. § 25 (2) PBefG

Trips without a licence

You do not need a licence for car trips that are free of charge or where the total payment is at most 30 cents per km (see lesson 1.1). Pure intermediaries of trips do not need a licence either § 2 (1b) PBefG.

Further exceptions are listed in the Exemption Regulation (Freistellungs-Verordnung) to the PBefG, for example trips off public roads or certain trips of workers to changing workplaces. They hardly matter for your taxi or Mietwagen business.

Typical mistakes

!“For 3 taxis I need 3 × 2.250 € = 6.750 € equity.”2.250 € only for the first vehicle, 1.250 € for each further one. So 4.750 €.
!“The certificate from the tax office may be a year old.”At most 3 months. One year applies to the certificate of equity capital.
!“I take the exam at the IHK where my business is.”The IHK where you live is responsible.
!“The licence has no time limit.”Maximum 5 years; for a new taxi applicant first 2 years.
!“The licence document is in the office, that's enough.”The vehicle must carry the licence or a shortened official copy.
