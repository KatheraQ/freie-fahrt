Documents to carry and retention periods

At a check by the police, customs or public order office, the driver must be able to show certain documents immediately. If something is missing, there are fines, for the driver and often for you as the operator. This lesson is short but popular in the exam: which document belongs to the driver, which to the vehicle, which to the company?

Exam area 2.9 · Documents to carryapprox. 20 minutes§ 17 (4) PBefG§ 48 (3) FeV§ 2a SchwarzArbG§§ 10, 27 BOKraftFZVDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • name all documents that must be in a taxi or Mietwagen on every trip
  • sort them by driver, vehicle and company
  • know the vehicle marking with licence number and operator's address
  • repeat the most important retention periods

The checklist for every trip

DocumentRelates toLegal basis
Driving licence (Führerschein, class B)driver§ 4 FeV
Passenger transport driving permit (P-Schein), together with the licence, hand over on requestdriver§ 48 (3) FeV
ID card, passport or substitute document, show to customs on requestdriver§ 2a SchwarzArbG
Registration certificate part I (Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I), on paper or digitally in the i-Kfz appvehicleFZV
Licence document or shortened official copy, in writing or electronicallycompany§ 17 (4) PBefG
Rules on fares and conditions of carriage, in a taxi the valid taxi tariff; passengers may look at itcompany§ 10 BOKraft
For Mietwagen: proof that the booking came via the registered office (e.g. in the app or on the booking note)company§ 49 (4) PBefG
Memory aid

Licence, P-Schein, ID, Registration, Permit (Genehmigung), Tariff. In German: F-P-A-Z-G-T (Führerschein, P-Schein, Ausweis, Zulassung, Genehmigung, Tarif).

Not documents, but also mandatory in the vehicle: first-aid kit, warning triangle, high-visibility vest and in taxis the child seats (lesson 3.3, lesson 1.5).

Marking on the vehicle

TaxiMietwagen
Licence number Ordnungsnummersign with the licence number issued by the authority at the bottom right of the rear window, readable from inside and outsidealso at the bottom right of the rear window (own design)
Operator's addresssign with the name and registered office of the operator inside, clearly visible to the passengernot required

§ 27 BOKraft

This way a passenger can identify the vehicle, for example if he has forgotten something or wants to complain.

Digital instead of paper

  • The licence or its shortened copy may also be carried electronically § 17 (4) PBefG.
  • Since late 2025 the vehicle registration certificate is also available digitally in the government i-Kfz app. Showing it in the app fulfils the duty to carry it.
  • Driving licence, P-Schein and ID must still be carried as originals.
In practice

Keep a folder for each vehicle with the licence copy, tariff and registration certificate. At every driver change, check that the driver has his licence, P-Schein and ID.

Repetition: how long to keep?

Booking records in Mietwagen transport1 year
Working time records (MiLoG, ArbZG)2 years
Business letters6 years
Accounting vouchers (receipts, invoices, taximeter data)8 years
Books, cash book, annual accounts10 years

Typical mistakes

!“The licence document is in the office.”In the vehicle: the document or a shortened official copy.
!“A photo of the P-Schein on my phone is enough.”The P-Schein must be carried and handed over as an original.
!“The driver doesn't need his ID, he has his driving licence.”In the passenger transport trade the ID duty under § 2a SchwarzArbG applies.

Summary
