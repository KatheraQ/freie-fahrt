Vehicle equipment and design

A taxi is more than a car with a yellow sign. BOKraft states exactly how taxis and Mietwagen must be built and equipped: doors, alarm system, markings, measuring device, navigation and luggage space. On top of that comes the general mandatory equipment of every car. The exam often asks what a taxi has and a Mietwagen doesn't.

Exam area 3.3 · Equipment and designapprox. 25 minutes§§ 17–31 BOKraft§§ 35h, 36, 53a StVZO§ 2 (3a) StVODeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • list the special equipment of taxis under BOKraft
  • compare the equipment of taxi and Mietwagen
  • name the general mandatory equipment of every car
  • know the rules on tyres and winter equipment

Of the special rules, only §§ 25, 27 and 30 BOKraft apply to Mietwagen § 31 BOKraft. The federal states can allow exceptions for some points, for example the colour § 43 BOKraft.

One vehicle for both?

If a vehicle has a taxi licence and also a licence for another type of transport, the taxi rules §§ 25 to 30 apply. While it is driving as a Mietwagen, the taxi sign must not be used § 31 BOKraft.

Mandatory equipment for every car

First-aid kitaccording to DIN 13164, not expired§ 35h StVZO
Warning triangletype-approved§ 53a (1) StVZO
High-visibility vestat least one, within reach§ 53a (2) StVZO

For taxis the child seats are also required (at least for 2 children from 9 kg, one of them for 9 to 18 kg, see lesson 1.5). A fire extinguisher is mandatory for buses but not for cars; it is still recommended.

Tyres and winter equipment

  • Minimum tread depth: 1,6 mm § 36 (2) StVZO. Recommended: 3 mm in summer, 4 mm in winter, because the braking distance gets much longer with little tread.
  • Situational winter tyre duty: in black ice, snow, slush, ice or frost, drive only with winter-suitable tyres § 2 (3a) StVO.
  • Since 1 October 2024 only tyres with the Alpine symbol (mountain with snowflake) count as winter-suitable. Pure M+S tyres are no longer enough.
  • BOKraft also requires the equipment to suit road and weather conditions. If necessary: winter tyres, snow chains, tow rope § 18 BOKraft.
Liability

Anyone who has an accident on ice with summer tyres risks a fine and trouble with the comprehensive insurer (gross negligence). As the keeper you must not order or allow trips with unsuitable tyres either.

Typical mistakes

!“Mietwagen don't need an alarm system.”§ 25 BOKraft applies to taxis and Mietwagen.
!“A partition is mandatory in a taxi.”It is allowed but not mandatory. If fitted, it must be bullet-resistant.
!“Minimum tread 3 mm.”By law 1,6 mm; 3–4 mm are recommendations.
!“M+S is enough in winter.”Since October 2024 only tyres with the Alpine symbol.
