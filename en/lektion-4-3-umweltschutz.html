Environmental protection

Taxis and Mietwagen drive a lot, mostly in town. That causes exhaust fumes and noise. As an operator you must know the basic rules of environmental protection: low-emission zones, unnecessary noise, correct handling of used oil, washing and waste. Protecting the environment often saves money too, above all on fuel.

After this lesson you can

  • explain low-emission zones and stickers
  • name rules against unnecessary noise and exhaust fumes
  • know the correct handling of used oil, tyres, batteries and washing water
  • order measures for economical and eco-friendly driving

Exhaust fumes and low-emission zones

  • Vehicles must meet the emission rules. This is checked with the emissions test during the general inspection § 47 StVZO.
  • In low-emission zones (Umweltzone, traffic sign 270.1) only vehicles with the right sticker (Plakette) may drive. The sticker shows the pollutant group 35. BImSchV.
  • The basis for low-emission zones and clean air plans are the limits for air quality 39. BImSchV.
  • The licensing authority can set requirements for taxis and Mietwagen on exhaust and emissions and make licences depend on them §§ 13 (5b), 64b PBefG.
Aachen

Aachen has a low-emission zone. Only vehicles with a green sticker may drive there (as of 2026). An exemption from the sticker duty has been decided for pure electric cars; check the current status with the City of Aachen.

Avoid noise and unnecessary exhaust fumes

Forbidden are § 30 (1) StVO:

  • unnecessary noise and avoidable exhaust nuisance,
  • letting engines run unnecessarily, for example at the taxi rank or while waiting for a passenger,
  • slamming doors too loudly,
  • pointless driving back and forth in built-up areas if this disturbs others.
In practice at the taxi rank

Engine off while waiting. Be quiet at night, radio low, close doors quietly. Otherwise residents complain to the authority, and that can cause trouble under the local taxi regulations.

Used oil, tyres, batteries, washing water

SubstanceCorrect disposal
Used oilOil changes only in a workshop or at suitable places. Anyone who sells engine oil must take back the same amount of used oil free of charge. Never put used oil into the sewer, the ground or household waste KrWG, Altölverordnung.
Washing waterDon't wash vehicles on the street or on unpaved ground. Oil, fuel and cleaning agents would get into the groundwater. Car washes have an oil separator WHG.
Old tyresReturn them to the dealer or a disposal company, never dump them.
Starter batteriesReturn them to the dealer (deposit). Not into household waste.
Air-conditioning refrigerantOnly a specialist workshop with certified staff.

Driving economically and eco-friendly

Fuel is one of the biggest variable cost items. Economical driving protects the environment and your wallet:

Drive with foresightTake your foot off the gas early, let the car roll, brake little.
Low engine speedShift up early. With an automatic, accelerate gently.
Tyre pressureToo little air increases consumption and wear. Check regularly.
Ballast and roof racksRemove unnecessary load from the boot. Use air conditioning only when needed.
Engine offWhen waiting, at the rank and during longer stops.
Choice of vehicleHybrid and electric vehicles reduce consumption, noise and exhaust in town. Electric cars are exempt from vehicle tax (lesson 1.10).
Calculation (example values)
Mileage 70.000 km, consumption falls through training from 7,0 to 6,3 l/100 km Saving = 70.000 ÷ 100 × 0,7 l = 490 l 490 l × 1,55 €/l net = 759,50 € per year and vehicle

Typical mistakes

!“In winter I leave the engine running at the rank for the heating.”Running the engine unnecessarily is forbidden (§ 30 StVO).
!“I quickly wash the taxi in front of my house.”In a car wash with an oil separator.
!“Used oil can go into general waste if it's in a canister.”Return it to the seller or a disposal company.
