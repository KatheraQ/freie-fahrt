Hosting on GitHub Pages

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access and the page requested, in order to deliver the website and keep the service secure. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is providing the website securely and reliably. More information: GitHub privacy statement.

Learning progress in your browser (local storage)

So that you can continue later, the website stores your progress (lessons done, answers, flashcards, mock exam results) and your settings (language, light/dark) in your browser's local storage. This data does not leave your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time on “My progress” or in your browser settings. The legal basis is § 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG, because storage is strictly necessary for the function you requested.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from the same server as the website. There is no connection to Google.

Contact by email

If you send an email, we use your details only to answer your request (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) and delete the email when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.

Freie Fahrt

Free preparation for the German IHK exam for taxi and private hire (Mietwagen) operators.

All texts and practice questions were written by us, based on German law, Annex 3 of the PBZugV and the DIHK syllabus. They are not original IHK exam questions. The IHK may use different wording and calculation layouts. This is not legal advice. The exam itself is in German, so key terms are always shown in German as well. All amounts are net unless stated otherwise.
