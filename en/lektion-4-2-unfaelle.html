Preventing accidents and acting correctly

If you drive a lot, you will have an accident at some point. What matters is that driver and operator then act correctly: secure the scene, help, allow the facts to be established, report. Afterwards you ask yourself: how do I prevent the next accident? Robberies of taxi drivers are also part of accident prevention.

Exam area 4.2 · Accident preventionapprox. 25 minutes§ 34 StVO§§ 142, 323c StGB§ 193 SGB VIIDGUV Vorschrift 1 and 70Deutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • describe the correct behaviour at the scene of an accident in the right order
  • know leaving the scene of an accident and failure to render assistance as criminal offences
  • know the duty to report to the Berufsgenossenschaft
  • draw up an emergency plan for the business
  • order measures against accidents and robberies

Behaviour at the scene of an accident

  1. Stop immediately, hazard lights on § 34 (1) No. 1 StVO.
  2. Protect yourself: put on the high-visibility vest, let passengers get out on the safe side and take them behind the crash barrier.
  3. Secure the scene: set up the warning triangle (in town about 50 m, on country roads about 100 m, on the motorway at least 150–200 m before the scene) § 34 (1) No. 2 StVO.
  4. Emergency call 112 if someone is injured: Where? What happened? How many injured? What injuries? Wait for questions.
  5. Give first aid § 34 (1) No. 4 StVO. Anyone who does not help although it is reasonable commits a criminal offence § 323c StGB.
  6. Allow the facts to be established: give your name, address and insurance, show your driving licence and registration certificate on request § 34 (1) No. 5 StVO. Call the police if people are injured, the situation is unclear, there is an argument or alcohol is suspected.
  7. Document: photos, witnesses, accident report (a European accident report in the glove box is useful).
  8. Report: to the operator, the insurer (without delay, a contractual duty) and, if the driver is injured, possibly the Berufsgenossenschaft.
Leaving the scene of an accident

Anyone who leaves the scene after an accident without allowing the facts to be established or waiting a reasonable time commits leaving the scene of an accident (Unfallflucht). This is a criminal offence § 142 StGB. Consequences: fine or prison, often loss of the driving licence and with it the P-Schein, recourse by the insurer up to 5.000 €. This also applies to small dents, for example a parking bump.

Reporting to the Berufsgenossenschaft

  • A work accident is an accident at work or on the direct way to and from work (commuting accident).
  • The operator must report it to the Berufsgenossenschaft if the insured person dies or is unable to work for more than 3 days. Deadline: within 3 days after the operator learns of it § 193 (1) and (4) SGB VII.
  • An injured driver goes to a D-Arzt (accident insurance doctor) if he is unable to work for more than one day or needs further medical treatment.
  • Report fatal accidents and accidents with many injured to the BG immediately, for example by phone.

Preventing accidents: learn from every accident

The exam expects you to be able to work out measures so that accidents and serious offences don't happen again. A four-step approach:

  1. Analyse: What happened, and why? Time, place, weather, driver, vehicle condition, shift length.
  2. Decide on measures, in this order: technical (e.g. emergency brake assist, winter tyres), organisational (shift plan, breaks, routes), personal (training, driver safety training).
  3. Instruct: inform all drivers about the case and the measures, document it.
  4. Check: Do the measures work? Keep accident statistics for the business.

Basis: DGUV Vorschrift 1 “Principles of prevention” (risk assessment, instruction, first aiders) and DGUV Vorschrift 70 “Vehicles” (inspection, operation).

Serious offences

Repeated serious offences by your drivers (alcohol, leaving the scene, working time) can call your own reliability as an operator into question if you do nothing about them (lesson 1.2).

Protection against robberies

Taxi drivers are robbed more often than many other professions. Measures:

TechnologyAlarm system under § 25 BOKraft, possibly a partition, emergency button with location via the dispatch centre or app, camera (only in compliance with data protection, with a notice sign).
Little cashEncourage card payment, remove cash regularly, keep only small change in the vehicle.
BehaviourWhen threatened, don't resist, hand over the money. Your own safety comes first. Remember the offender, then call the police immediately.
AftercareA robbery is a work accident. Psychological first aid, D-Arzt, report to the BG.

Typical mistakes

!“If I scratch a parked car, I leave a note and drive on.”That can be leaving the scene of an accident. Wait a reasonable time, otherwise call the police.
!“Accident report to the BG: within 4 weeks.”Within 3 days if unable to work for more than 3 days.
!“Put the warning triangle directly behind the car.”With distance: town about 50 m, country road about 100 m, motorway at least 150–200 m.
