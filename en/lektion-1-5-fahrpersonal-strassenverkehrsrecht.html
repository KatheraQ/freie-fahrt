Drivers and road traffic law

As an operator you are responsible for making sure only drivers with the right permit drive. On top of that come special traffic rules for taxis: child seats, seat belts, bus lanes, alcohol and smoking. Many exam questions are about the “P-Schein” and the child seat rule in taxis.

Exam area 1.3 · Road traffic lawapprox. 30 minutes§ 48 FeV§§ 21, 21a, 23 StVO§ 8 BOKraftBNichtrSchGDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • explain who needs a passenger transport driving permit
  • name the requirements for issuing and renewing it
  • know the operator's duties as vehicle keeper
  • apply the child seat rule for taxis correctly
  • know the rules on seat belts, bus lanes, alcohol and smoking in taxis

The passenger transport driving permit

Anyone who carries passengers in a taxi, Mietwagen or other vehicle requiring a PBefG licence needs a passenger transport driving permit (Fahrerlaubnis zur Fahrgastbeförderung) in addition to the normal driving licence. Colloquially it is called the “P-Schein” § 48 (1) FeV.

This also applies to you as an operator if you drive yourself. Holders of a bus licence class D or D1 do not need a P-Schein § 48 (2) No. 4 FeV.

§ 48 (4) FeV

Validity and renewal

  • The P-Schein is valid for at most 5 years.
  • On application it is extended by up to 5 years each time if you again prove health, eyesight and reliability.
  • From the age of 60 you also need proof of your performance (e.g. resilience, reaction) for a renewal.

§ 48 (5) and (6) FeV

Knowledge test instead of local knowledge test

Taxi drivers used to have to pass a local knowledge test (Ortskundeprüfung). It was abolished with the PBefG reform in August 2021. Instead § 48 FeV provides for proof of professional knowledge for drivers of taxis, Mietwagen and pooled on-demand transport, often called the “small knowledge test” (kleine Fachkunde). How and when it is required is decided by the federal states. Permits already issued remain valid. Ask your driving licence authority for the current situation.

Don't confuse them

The operator's exam (Fachkundeprüfung für Unternehmer, this exam at the IHK) and the driver's knowledge test (for the P-Schein) are two different things.

Your duties as an operator

  • As the vehicle keeper (Halter) you may not order or allow passenger transport if the driver has no valid P-Schein § 48 (7) FeV.
  • The driver must carry the P-Schein together with the driving licence and hand it over at checks § 48 (3) FeV.
  • The authority revokes the P-Schein if a requirement is no longer met, for example after serious crimes § 48 (9) FeV.
Practical tip

Make a copy of every P-Schein and driving licence, note the expiry date and check the originals regularly. That way you notice in time when a permit expires.

Conduct while on duty

Drivers must behave considerately and calmly § 8 (1) BOKraft. For taxi and Mietwagen drivers also § 8 (3) and (5) BOKraft:

  • No alcohol or other intoxicating substances while on duty or on standby. Anyone under their influence may not start a trip. This is stricter than the general 0.5 per mille limit.
  • No watching TV while driving.

Smoking is forbidden in taxis and Mietwagen. They count as public passenger transport §§ 1, 2 No. 2 BNichtrSchG.

The driver may not hold the phone while driving. Using it in a holder or hands-free is allowed if only a short glance is needed § 23 (1a) StVO.

Carrying children safely

Children under 12 years who are smaller than 150 cm may only travel in a suitable, approved child restraint (child seat or booster seat) § 21 (1a) StVO.

The special rule for taxis

A taxi does not know in advance how many children will get in. So taxis (and other cars with an obligation to carry) have an easier rule:

Taxi rule
  • On the rear seats only 2 children weighing at least 9 kg must be able to be secured.
  • Of these, at least 1 child weighing 9 to 18 kg must be able to be secured.
  • This relief does not apply if children are carried regularly, for example on school runs. Then the full duty applies.

In practice: a taxi should always carry at least one child seat for 9 to 18 kg and a second restraint (for example a booster seat).

Another exception: children from 3 years may be secured with the normal belt on the rear seat if there is no space for another child seat because of other child seats § 21 (1a) StVO.

Who is responsible?

The driver is responsible for securing children correctly. Adult passengers must fasten their own seat belt.

Seat belts and bus lanes

Seat belts also for taxi drivers

Taxi drivers used to be allowed to drive without a seat belt when carrying passengers. This exception no longer exists. Today taxi and Mietwagen drivers must also wear a seat belt while driving § 21a (1) StVO.

Bus lanes

A bus lane (sign 245, “BUS”) is for scheduled buses. Taxis may only use it if an additional sign allows it, for example “Taxi frei”. Without such a sign the bus lane is off-limits for taxis too.

Typical mistakes

!“At 18 with a class B licence I can drive a taxi.”Minimum age 21 and class B for at least 2 years, plus the P-Schein.
!“The P-Schein is valid forever.”At most 5 years, then renewal with new proof.
!“Taxi drivers don't have to wear a seat belt.”This exception was abolished. Seat belts are mandatory.
!“I don't need a child seat in a taxi.”Restraints for at least 2 children from 9 kg, one of them 9 to 18 kg, must be possible.
!“0.3 per mille is fine on duty.”On duty: no alcohol (§ 8 BOKraft).
