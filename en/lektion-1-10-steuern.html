Taxes: VAT, receipts, vehicle, income and trade tax

Taxes are a big part of the exam. The most important is VAT (Umsatzsteuer): when does 7 % apply, when 19 %? What must be on a receipt? Then there are income, trade, vehicle and wage taxes. Many candidates calculate VAT wrongly in the exam. We practise that here.

Exam area 1.8 · Tax lawapprox. 40 minutes§§ 12, 14, 15, 18, 19 UStG§§ 33, 34 UStDV§ 146a AO, KassenSichV§§ 32a, 35, 37 EStG§§ 11, 16 GewStGKraftStGDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • determine the correct VAT rate for taxi and Mietwagen trips
  • extract VAT from a gross amount and calculate the VAT payable
  • know the required details on receipts and invoices
  • explain the small business rule, VAT returns and the duty to issue receipts
  • explain income, trade, vehicle and wage tax in outline and calculate trade tax

VAT: 7 % or 19 %?

The standard rate of 19 % applies to transport in principle § 12 (1) UStG. For taxis there is a reduced rate of 7 % if the trip § 12 (2) No. 10 UStG:

  • stays within one municipality, or
  • the distance is not more than 50 km.

Mietwagen are not named in this provision. So the standard rate of 19 % applies to them, even for short trips. An exception exists for patient transport carried out by a Mietwagen on the same terms as a taxi, for example under an agreement with a health insurer that also applies to taxis.

TripVAT rate
Taxi within Aachen, 12 km7 %
Taxi from Aachen to Düren, 34 km (different municipality, but ≤ 50 km)7 %
Taxi from Aachen to Cologne, 72 km19 %
Mietwagen within Aachen, 8 km19 %
Mietwagen to Düsseldorf airport, 85 km19 %
Remember

7 % only for taxis, and only within the municipality or up to 50 km. Mietwagen: 19 %.

Calculating VAT

The taxi tariff is a gross price. The VAT is already included. You must extract it:

VAT = gross amount × rate ÷ (100 + rate)
Net = gross amount ÷ (1 + rate)
Example
Taxi trip in town, passenger pays 21,40 € VAT = 21,40 € × 7 ÷ 107 = 1,40 € Net = 21,40 € ÷ 1,07 = 20,00 € Mietwagen trip, customer pays 59,50 € VAT = 59,50 € × 19 ÷ 119 = 9,50 € Net = 59,50 € ÷ 1,19 = 50,00 €
Most common mistake

21,40 € × 7 % = 1,50 € is wrong. The 7 % refers to the net amount, not the gross amount. That is why you calculate × 7 ÷ 107.

Input VAT and VAT payable

When you buy things yourself (fuel, repairs, a new car), you also pay VAT. You may deduct this input VAT (Vorsteuer) from your own VAT if you have a proper invoice § 15 UStG. You only pay the difference to the tax office, the VAT payable (Zahllast).

VAT payable = VAT collected − input VAT paid
Example (sample values, one quarter)
VAT from taxi trips (7 %) 2.450,00 € − input VAT on fuel (19 %) − 760,00 € − input VAT on garage work (19 %) − 190,00 € = VAT payable to the tax office 1.500,00 €

Because taxis often collect only 7 % but pay 19 % input VAT on purchases, months with big purchases can even lead to a refund.

VAT returns and small businesses

Advance VAT return

  • You report VAT electronically (ELSTER) and pay it by the 10th day after the end of the period § 18 (1) UStG.
  • The period is normally the calendar quarter. If the tax in the previous year was over 9.000 €, you file monthly. If it was at most 2.000 €, the tax office can release you from advance returns § 18 (2) UStG.
  • On application there is a permanent extension (Dauerfristverlängerung) by one month.
  • After the year ends you file an annual VAT return.

Small business rule

With little turnover you can be a small business (Kleinunternehmer). Since 2025: turnover in the previous year at most 25.000 € and in the current year at most 100.000 € § 19 UStG.

  • A small business charges no VAT and pays none.
  • But it may also not deduct input VAT.
  • It can waive the rule. Then it is bound for 5 years.

For a taxi this means: the tariff is a final price. A small business keeps the full fare. For a full-time business, however, the limit is usually exceeded quickly.

Receipt, invoice, till

The taxi receipt (up to 250 €)

For tax purposes a taxi receipt is a small-amount invoice (Kleinbetragsrechnung) if the amount is at most 250 € gross. It must contain § 33 UStDV:

  1. full name and address of the operator
  2. date of issue
  3. type and scope of the service (e.g. “taxi trip from … to …”)
  4. fare and tax in one sum (i.e. the gross amount)
  5. tax rate (7 % or 19 %)

A taxi receipt is not a ticket (Fahrausweis) under § 34 UStDV. Tickets are, for example, train or bus tickets. Invoices for taxi or Mietwagen trips are not included.

Invoices over 250 €

From 250,01 € you need a full invoice with all details of § 14 (4) UStG, among others: name and address of you and the customer, your tax number or VAT ID, a sequential invoice number, date of service, net amount, tax rate and tax amount shown separately.

E-invoice

Between businesses (B2B) the electronic invoice is becoming mandatory step by step. Since 2025 all businesses must be able to receive e-invoices. Small-amount invoices up to 250 € are exempt. So a normal taxi receipt is still possible.

The taximeter as a till: TSE and receipt duty

  • For tax purposes taximeters and odometers count as electronic recording systems. Since 1 January 2026 every device must have a certified technical security device (TSE) § 146a AO, KassenSichV.
  • Duty to issue receipts: for every trip a receipt must be created and offered to the passenger, on paper or electronically § 146a (2) AO.
  • You must report the systems to the tax office via ELSTER.
  • A breach is a tax offence.

Income tax

As a sole proprietor you pay income tax on your profit, not on turnover. You usually determine the profit with cash-basis accounting (lesson 2.4).

  • Basic tax-free allowance 2026: 12.348 €. Up to this amount you pay no income tax § 32a EStG.
  • The rate rises with income, from 14 % to 42 % (45 % for very high incomes).
  • The tax office sets advance payments. They are due on 10 March, 10 June, 10 September and 10 December § 37 EStG.

A GmbH or UG pays corporation tax (15 % plus solidarity surcharge) and trade tax instead of income tax.

Trade tax

Trade tax goes to the municipality. It is calculated like this:

  1. Trade income (simplified: the profit), rounded down to full 100 €
  2. minus allowance of 24.500 € (only for sole proprietorships and partnerships, not for GmbH) § 11 (1) GewStG
  3. × base rate 3,5 % = tax base amount (Steuermessbetrag) § 11 (2) GewStG
  4. × municipal multiplier (Hebesatz) = trade tax § 16 GewStG
Example (sample values, multiplier 450 %)
Profit (trade income) 52.380 € → rounded down 52.300 € − allowance − 24.500 € = remaining 27.800 € × 3,5 % = tax base amount 973 € × 450 % multiplier = trade tax 4.378,50 € Credit against income tax: 4,0 × 973 € = 3.892 € (at most the trade tax actually paid)

Sole proprietors get relief: 4 times the tax base amount is deducted from income tax § 35 EStG. With a multiplier around 400 %, trade tax is almost fully offset this way.

Vehicle tax and wage tax

Vehicle tax

  • For cars with a combustion engine it depends on engine capacity and CO₂ emissions. Diesel costs more per 100 cm³ than petrol.
  • Electric cars are exempt: for 10 years from first registration if first registered by 31.12.2030, at most until 31.12.2035 § 3d KraftStG.
  • Customs (main customs office) is responsible. Payment is by direct debit, usually once a year.

Wage tax

For employed drivers you withhold wage tax (and, where applicable, solidarity surcharge and church tax) from the gross wage. You retrieve the tax data (tax class) electronically (ELStAM). You report wage tax with the wage tax return and pay it by the 10th of the following month to the tax office § 41a EStG.

Typical mistakes

!“Mietwagen also pay 7 %.”7 % only for taxis (municipality or ≤ 50 km). Mietwagen: 19 %.
!VAT from 107 € calculated as 107 × 7 % = 7,49 €.107 × 7 ÷ 107 = 7,00 €.
!“A taxi receipt is a ticket (Fahrausweis).”Invoices for taxi and Mietwagen trips are not tickets. Up to 250 €: small-amount invoice.
!“Trade tax: the 24.500 € allowance also for a GmbH.”Only for natural persons and partnerships.
!“I pay income tax on my turnover.”On profit.
