IHK exam for taxi and private hire operators · Lesson 4.1

Road safety and vehicle checks

As an operator you are responsible for the safety of your vehicles and drivers. The exam expects you to be able to write instructions for your drivers: What does the driver check before the shift? How does he drive safely? What does he do when tired? This also includes a few rules of thumb for the stopping distance.

Exam area 4.1 · Road safetyapprox. 25 minutesDGUV Vorschrift 70 (§§ 36, 57)DGUV Grundsatz 314-002DGUV Vorschrift 1§ 12 ArbSchG§§ 3, 4, 23 StVODeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • draw up a pre-departure check for drivers
  • know the inspection duties under DGUV Vorschrift 70
  • calculate reaction distance, braking distance and stopping distance with rules of thumb
  • name the biggest dangers in everyday taxi work and order countermeasures
  • know the duty to instruct drivers

The pre-departure check

Under the accident prevention regulation “Vehicles” the driver must check before every shift whether the most important safety equipment works. He reports defects immediately. If a defect endangers safety, he must not drive off § 36 DGUV Vorschrift 70. What exactly to check is described in DGUV Grundsatz 314-002.

AreaCheck points
Walk around outsidetyres (pressure, tread, damage), lights and indicators, mirrors, windows clean and without cracks, number plates, taxi sign, licence number, accident damage
Inside the vehiclebrakes (pedal travel, parking brake), steering, horn, wipers and washers, warning lights on the display, seat belts, child seats, alarm system
Equipmentfirst-aid kit, warning triangle, high-visibility vest, taximeter/distance meter works and is calibrated, papers complete
Fluidsfuel or charge level, oil, coolant, washer fluid (regularly, not every shift)

In addition, the operator has the vehicles checked for safe operating condition by a competent person at least once a year and records the result in writing § 57 DGUV Vorschrift 70. For cars, according to the Berufsgenossenschaften, a defect-free service at a specialist workshop can be enough if it also covers occupational safety.

Stopping distance: the rules of thumb

Reaction distance (m) = (speed in km/h ÷ 10) × 3
Braking distance (m) = (speed in km/h ÷ 10)²
Braking distance in an emergency stop (m) = (speed ÷ 10)² ÷ 2
Stopping distance = reaction distance + braking distance
km/h

Driving school rules of thumb for a dry road. On wet or icy roads or when tired the distance gets much longer.

What does this mean?

At 50 km/h a car needs about 40 m with normal braking, at 100 km/h already 130 m. Double the speed means four times the braking distance. In the city with many pedestrians every km/h less saves lives.

The biggest dangers in everyday taxi work

DangerInstruction to drivers
Tiredness, microsleepTake breaks (ArbZG), stop immediately when tired. Coffee does not replace sleep. Plan and limit long night shifts.
DistractionDon't hold the phone (§ 23 StVO). Set navigation and radio before driving. Take payment only when stationary.
Too fast, too closeAdapt speed to traffic and weather (§ 3 StVO); distance outside towns at least “half the speedometer” in metres (§ 4 StVO).
Alcohol, drugs, medicationAbsolutely forbidden on duty (§ 8 BOKraft). With medication, read the package leaflet.
Getting in and outLet passengers get out on the pavement side if possible, watch for cyclists (open doors slowly), avoid stopping in the second row with hazard lights: better look for a safe place to stop.
LuggageHeavy luggage in the boot, not loose on the back seat or parcel shelf. In an emergency stop it becomes a projectile.
Wheelchair transportSecure the wheelchair and the person with the prescribed restraint systems.

Instructing the drivers

  • The employer must instruct employees on safety and health (Unterweisung): when hiring, when something changes and after that at least once a year § 12 ArbSchG, § 4 DGUV Vorschrift 1.
  • The instruction is documented (date, content, drivers' signatures).
  • Content: pre-departure check, safe driving, behaviour in accidents and robberies, first aid, dealing with difficult passengers, child seats, securing wheelchairs.
  • The basis is the risk assessment (Gefährdungsbeurteilung): What dangers exist in my business, and what do I do about them? § 5 ArbSchG
  • Sensible: driver safety training. Many Berufsgenossenschaften subsidise it.

Typical mistakes

!“The garage does the pre-departure check once a year.”The driver does it before every shift. Additionally a competent person once a year.
!Braking distance at 100 km/h = 2 × braking distance at 50 km/h.Four times as long: (100 ÷ 10)² = 100 m instead of 25 m.
!“One instruction when hiring is enough.”Repeat at least yearly and document it.
