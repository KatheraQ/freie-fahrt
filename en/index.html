Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Who needs this exam (Fachkundeprüfung)?

Anyone who wants to run a taxi or private hire (Mietwagen) business in Germany needs a licence under the Passenger Transport Act (PBefG). For this, the owner or the manager must prove professional competence (fachliche Eignung), usually by passing this exam at the Chamber of Commerce (IHK). This also applies if you only have one car and drive for Uber or Bolt.

Employed drivers do not need this exam. They need the passenger transport driving permit (Fahrerlaubnis zur Fahrgastbeförderung, “P-Schein”).

Can I take the exam in English?

No. The exam is held in German. That is why this course shows all key terms in German and every question also in its German original. Ask your IHK whether a dictionary is allowed.

How does the exam work?

Usually there are two written parts of 60 minutes each. If your written score is not quite enough, you may still pass in an oral exam. Details differ between IHKs. Everything is explained in lesson 6.1.

Are these real exam questions?

No. The IHK does not publish its questions. All questions here were written by us, based on the law and the official syllabus. They train the same knowledge, but the wording in the exam will be different.

Do I need an account? Where is my progress stored?

No account needed. Your progress is stored only in your browser. On My progress you can save it as a code and load it on another device.

How long does it take?

The lessons take about 14 hours in total. Add time for practice, especially cost accounting. Many people study for 4 to 8 weeks next to work.
