IHK exam for taxi and private hire operators · Lesson 1.8

Social insurance

As an employer you register every driver for social insurance and pay contributions. The taxi trade has a particularly strict rule: the immediate notification (Sofortmeldung) before the first working day. And you yourself as an operator also have duties, for example towards the Berufsgenossenschaft.

Exam area 1.5 · Social insurance lawapprox. 30 minutesSGB IV, V, VI, VII, XI, SGB III§ 28a SGB IV (notifications)DEÜVDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • name the five branches of social insurance with their providers and who pays
  • explain the employer's notification duties, especially the immediate notification
  • know when and how contributions are paid
  • tell mini-jobs and the transition zone apart
  • know the operator's own social insurance and the risk of bogus self-employment

Contributions are only calculated up to the contribution ceiling (Beitragsbemessungsgrenze). 2026: health and care 5.812,50 € per month, pension and unemployment 8.450 € per month.

In addition there are levies paid only by the employer: U1 (refund for sickness, for businesses with usually up to 30 employees), U2 (refund for maternity, all businesses) and the insolvency levy.

Remember

Health, care, pension and unemployment insurance are paid jointly by employer and employee. Accident insurance is paid by the employer alone.

Notifications: immediate notification and registration

The employer registers employees with the health insurer. It is the collecting agency (Einzugsstelle) for all contributions except accident insurance. For mini-jobs the Minijob-Zentrale is responsible. First you need an employer number (Betriebsnummer) from the Federal Employment Agency.

The immediate notification

In the passenger transport trade you must report every new employee directly to the pension insurance data office at the latest when employment starts. This is the immediate notification (Sofortmeldung) § 28a (4) SGB IV. It applies to the same sectors as the ID duty under § 2a SchwarzArbG.

Why so strict?

At a customs check nobody should be able to say: “He's only starting today, the registration is coming.” Missing the immediate notification can lead to a fine.

The normal registration

In addition you register the employee with the health insurer with the first payroll, at the latest within 6 weeks of the start § 6 DEÜV. Further notifications follow when employment ends (deregistration) and once a year (annual notification).

Paying contributions

  • The employer deducts the employee's share from the wage and pays the total social insurance contribution to the health insurer.
  • It is due on the third-to-last bank working day of the month in which the work was done § 23 (1) SGB IV. You send the contribution statement electronically beforehand.
  • You pay accident insurance once a year to the Berufsgenossenschaft based on your payroll report.

And you yourself as the operator?

Accident insurance: mandatoryTaxi and Mietwagen operators are insured themselves with BG Verkehr under its statutes, even without employees. The Federal Constitutional Court has confirmed this duty. Register the business with the BG within 1 week § 192 SGB VII.
Health insurance: mandatoryEveryone must have health insurance, statutory (voluntary) or private. Long-term care insurance comes with it.
Pension insurance: usually voluntaryBut mandatory if you have no employees subject to insurance and work essentially for only one client (employee-like self-employed person) § 2 sentence 1 No. 9 SGB VI.
Unemployment insurance: voluntaryPossible on application if you were previously employed with insurance. Apply within 3 months of becoming self-employed § 28a SGB III.
Careful with app trips

If you drive alone with one Mietwagen and almost only take bookings from one single platform, have it checked whether you are subject to pension insurance as an employee-like self-employed person.

Bogus self-employment

Some operators use drivers as so-called “subcontractors” or “freelancers” to save on social contributions. If the driver actually works like an employee, he is bogus self-employed (scheinselbständig). Typical signs:

  • He drives your vehicle, under your licence.
  • You decide shifts and working hours.
  • He has no business risk of his own and no customers of his own.

Then he is an employee. You must pay the contributions retroactively, up to 4 years back, with intent up to 30 years, including the employee's share § 25 SGB IV. On top come late-payment surcharges and possibly criminal proceedings for withholding wages § 266a StGB. If in doubt, use the status determination procedure (Statusfeststellungsverfahren) at Deutsche Rentenversicherung § 7a SGB IV.

Typical mistakes

!“I'll register the driver after the first month.”In the taxi trade: immediate notification at the latest when work starts.
!“Accident insurance is paid half each.”Only by the employer, to the Berufsgenossenschaft.
!“As a sole operator I don't need the BG.”Taxi and Mietwagen operators are insured with BG Verkehr under its statutes.
!“My drivers are subcontractors, so I pay no contributions.”Anyone who works like an employee is an employee. Back payment up to 4 years, with intent up to 30 years.
