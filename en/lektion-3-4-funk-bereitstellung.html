Radio, standby and taxi ranks

How does the passenger get to the taxi? At the taxi rank, by radio from the dispatch centre or by app. Waiting at the rank has clear rules from the PBefG, the StVO and the local taxi regulations. For your own radio you need a frequency from the Federal Network Agency. And Mietwagen have the duty to return when they are on standby.

Exam area 3.7 / 3.9 · Telephone and radio / Vehicle standbyapprox. 25 minutes§§ 47, 49 PBefGStVO Annex 2 sign 229TKGTaxenordnungDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • name the rules for keeping taxis on standby
  • know traffic sign 229 “taxi rank” and its meaning
  • describe behaviour at the taxi rank under typical local taxi regulations
  • explain who assigns radio frequencies
  • classify dispatch by centre, radio and app

Where may a taxi wait for customers?

  • Taxis may only be kept on standby at places approved by the authority § 47 (1) PBefG.
  • In principle only in the municipality of the registered office. The authority can allow exceptions and define a larger area § 47 (2) PBefG.
  • Passengers may stop a free taxi on the street by hand signal. The taxi may also accept bookings during a trip.
  • Details of standby (order at the ranks, radio operation, service) are set by the state government or the municipality in the local taxi regulations (Taxenordnung) § 47 (3) PBefG.

Mietwagen may not wait for customers on public roads. They wait at the registered office or at approved parking places (lesson 1.1).

The taxi rank

Sign 229: taxi rank Taxenstand“Drivers must not stop at taxi ranks, except taxis that are kept ready for passenger transport.” Often with an additional sign showing how many taxis may stand there.StVO Annex 2, sign 229

This means: other cars may not even stop there briefly. A taxi may only stand there if it is ready for passengers, not to take a break or park.

Behaviour at the rank (typical rules of local taxi regulations)

  • Taxis line up in order of arrival and move up.
  • The driver stays with the vehicle and is ready to drive at any time.
  • The passenger has free choice: he may take any taxi at the rank, not only the first one.
  • No washing, repairing or loud music at the rank. Be considerate of residents.
  • If the rank is full, another taxi must not wait in the second row.

The exact rules are in the taxi regulations of your city or district. Breaches are administrative offences.

Radio, dispatch centre and app

In the past most bookings came via the taxi radio of a dispatch centre. Today a lot runs via data radio and apps. For the exam you should know:

  • Anyone who wants to run their own radio network (business radio) needs a frequency assignment from the Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur). The legal basis is the Telecommunications Act (TKG). The assignment costs fees.
  • Business radio frequencies are usually shared by several users, for a certain area (e.g. a city).
  • Radio devices must be approved (CE marking) and may only transmit on the assigned frequencies.
  • Usually an operator joins a taxi dispatch centre (Taxizentrale) and pays a monthly radio fee (fixed costs, lesson 2.2).
  • With app dispatch the operator usually pays a commission per trip (variable costs).
Mietwagen and radio

A booking for a Mietwagen by radio or app must also go via the registered office and be recorded there. Then the Mietwagen may accept a new booking during a trip (§ 49 (4) PBefG).

Typical mistakes

!“I may stop briefly at the taxi rank to let someone out.”Not with a normal car. Only taxis on standby may stop there.
!“The passenger must take the first taxi in the line.”He has free choice.
!“The IHK assigns radio frequencies.”The Federal Network Agency does.
