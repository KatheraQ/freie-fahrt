Maintenance, roadworthiness test and calibration

Taxis often drive 60.000 to 100.000 km a year. That wears out brakes, tyres and suspension quickly. This is why taxis and Mietwagen have shorter inspection intervals than private cars: a general inspection and taximeter calibration every year. As an operator you must also be able to draw up a maintenance plan.

Exam area 3.2 · Maintenance and inspectionapprox. 25 minutes§ 29 StVZO and Annex VIII§§ 41, 42 BOKraftMess- und Eichverordnung (MessEV)DGUV Vorschrift 70Deutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • name the intervals for general inspection and calibration
  • explain what is additionally checked under BOKraft during the general inspection
  • know when a device must be recalibrated
  • draw up a simple maintenance plan for a taxi

General inspection

The general inspection is called Hauptuntersuchung (HU), often simply “TÜV”.

VehicleHU every
private car24 months (new: first after 36)
Car for passenger transport under the PBefG (taxi, Mietwagen)12 months

§ 29 StVZO, Annex VIII No. 2.1

  • The HU is carried out by inspection engineers from TÜV, DEKRA, GTÜ, KÜS and other inspection organisations. The emissions test is part of the HU.
  • For taxis and Mietwagen the inspector also checks whether the vehicle complies with BOKraft: taxi sign, alarm system, doors, licence number, etc. § 41 BOKraft
  • The licensing authority receives a copy of the report without delay § 41 BOKraft.
  • Before the first use in the business an HU under BOKraft is required, even for a new car § 42 BOKraft.
  • If you exceed the deadline by more than 2 months, you get a more expensive extended inspection. Driving with an expired HU also costs a fine.

Calibration of taximeter and distance meter

DeviceCalibration interval
Taximeter (Fahrpreisanzeiger)1 year
Distance meter (Wegstreckenzähler, Mietwagen)2 years

MessEV Annex 7

The responsible body is the state calibration office (Eichamt). Apply in good time; many offices want the application several weeks before the deadline.

When must it be recalibrated earlier?

  • after a repair or any intervention in the device,
  • after reprogramming, for example after a tariff change,
  • when the device is installed in a different vehicle,
  • when the seal is damaged.
Without valid calibration

A taximeter without valid calibration must not be used. This breaks calibration law and BOKraft (§ 28 BOKraft: “the provisions of calibration law apply”).

A maintenance plan

The DIHK framework requires that you can draw up a maintenance plan for your vehicles. Plan maintenance by kilometres and by time, whichever comes first.

Keep a maintenance log or list for each vehicle: date, mileage, work done, next due date. This also helps when selling and in disputes with the insurer.

Why is it worth it?

A taxi that is off the road for a week because of a defect earns no money but still costs fixed costs. Preventive maintenance is usually cheaper than a breakdown.

Typical mistakes

!“My taxi has an HU every 2 years like a private car.”Taxi and Mietwagen: every 12 months.
!“The distance meter is calibrated every year.”Taximeter: 1 year. Distance meter: 2 years.
!“After the tariff change I can reset the taximeter myself.”A specialist workshop reprograms it, then it must be calibrated.
