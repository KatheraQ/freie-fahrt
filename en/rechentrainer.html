Tips for the exam
  • Write down every step with the formula. Even if the result is wrong, you often get points for the method.
  • Write the unit next to every number: €, €/km, km, %.
  • Round only at the end. Use at least 4 decimals for intermediate results.
  • Bring a non-programmable calculator. Ask your IHK which one is allowed.
