Documents for trips abroad

Which papers belong in the vehicle when you cross the border? In principle the same as at home, plus ID and a few useful extras. This short lesson sums it all up.

Exam area 5.3 · Transport documents abroadapprox. 15 minutes§ 17 (4) PBefG§ 48 FeVPflVG, Green CardExam area 5.3Deutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • name driver-, vehicle- and company-related documents for trips abroad
  • know the purpose of the Green Card and the European accident report

Checklist for trips abroad

Relates toDocumentNote
DriverID card or passportrequired at border checks; a driving licence is not an ID
Driving licenceEU driving licences are valid throughout the EU
P-Scheinrequired for transport under German law; abroad the other country's rules also apply
VehicleRegistration certificate part Ipaper or i-Kfz app; abroad paper is safer, because the app is not known everywhere
Proof of motor liability insurance (international motor insurance card, “Green Card”)Within the EU the number plate counts as proof of insurance; the card is still helpful after accidents and mandatory in some non-EU countries
European accident reporta standard form in all languages that makes reporting damage easier
CompanyLicence or shortened official copy§ 17 (4) PBefG; border police and customs can ask for it (§ 52 (4))
for Mietwagen: proof of bookingshows that the booking came via the registered office (important to avoid suspicion of cabotage)
Passengers

Passengers also need valid ID documents (lesson 5.2). Point this out when they book, for example for airport trips to Maastricht or Liège.

Equipment in neighbouring countries

Some countries have different equipment rules. In Belgium and the Netherlands the German mandatory equipment (warning triangle, first-aid kit, high-visibility vest) is usually enough. For trips further away, for example to France or Luxembourg, find out beforehand, for instance from the ADAC.

Summary

DriverID card/passport, driving licence, P-Schein
Vehicleregistration part I, Green Card (recommended), European accident report
Companylicence/copy; Mietwagen: proof of booking
