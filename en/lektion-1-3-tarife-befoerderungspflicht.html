Fares and the obligation to carry

In a taxi you cannot set the price yourself. It is in the taxi tariff (Taxitarif), and in principle you must take everyone. Since 2021 there are exceptions: fixed prices and price ranges for pre-booked trips. Here you learn who sets the tariff, what it contains, when you may refuse a trip and what applies to Mietwagen.

Exam area 1.1 · Passenger transport lawapprox. 30 minutes§§ 21, 22, 39, 47, 51, 51a PBefG§§ 13–15, 38–40 BOKraftDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • explain who sets the taxi tariff and what it can regulate
  • know the tariff obligation and its exceptions (fixed price, price range, special agreement)
  • explain the mandatory service area
  • name the three conditions of the obligation to carry
  • justify when a passenger may be refused or excluded
  • know the pricing rules for Mietwagen

Who sets the taxi tariff?

The PBefG allows the state government to set taxi fares by regulation (Rechtsverordnung). It can delegate this task § 51 (1) PBefG. In practice this is usually done by the districts and independent cities. The tariff is then published in a taxi tariff regulation, often together with the local taxi regulations (Taxenordnung).

Before the tariff is set or changed, the IHK, trade unions and transport associations, among others, are consulted § 51 (3) with § 14 (2) PBefG. Several authorities can agree on a common tariff for a larger area § 51 (4) PBefG.

What can the tariff contain?

The regulation can cover, among other things § 51 (1) sentence 2 PBefG:

Base fare GrundpreisA fixed amount for every trip.
Price per km KilometerpreisPrice per kilometre driven, often in steps.
Time price ZeitpreisFor waiting time and slow driving, e.g. in traffic jams.
Fixed prices FestpreiseFor certain routes, e.g. to the airport.
Surcharges ZuschlägeFor example for a large taxi or a lot of luggage.
PaymentAdvance payment, billing, means of payment (e.g. card payment).
Sample tariff (sample values)
Base fare4,50 €
1st to 5th km2,50 € per km
from the 6th km2,20 € per km
Waiting time36,00 € per hour

Trip of 8 km with 5 minutes in traffic:

Base fare = 4,50 € 5 km × 2,50 € = 12,50 € 3 km × 2,20 € = 6,60 € 5 min ÷ 60 × 36,00 € = 3,00 € Fare = 26,60 € (incl. 7 % VAT)

Taxi tariffs are final prices including VAT. More in lesson 1.10. More tasks in the calculation trainer.

The tariff obligation

The set tariff is binding. You may neither charge more nor less. Discounts are only allowed if they apply to everyone under the same conditions. Other discounts are forbidden and void § 51 (5) with § 39 (3) PBefG.

Examples
  • “10 % off for you as a regular customer.” Forbidden unless it is in the tariff.
  • “At night it costs 5 € more because I'm tired.” Forbidden if the tariff has no night surcharge.
  • Breaking the tariff obligation can cost a fine of up to 10.000 €. § 61 PBefG

The taxi charges with the calibrated taximeter § 28 BOKraft. The driver takes the shortest route unless the passenger wants something else. He may take another route if it is better for traffic or cheaper and the passenger agrees § 38 BOKraft.

The roof sign

Within the tariff area the taxi roof sign must be lit when the taxi is free. At a taxi rank it does not have to be lit. During a booked trip it is switched off § 39 BOKraft. So people on the street can see at once whether a taxi is free.

Exceptions to the tariff obligation

1. Fixed price or price range for pre-booked trips

Since the PBefG reform of 2021, the tariff can provide for pre-booked trips § 51 (1) sentence 4 PBefG:

  • fixed prices, or
  • a price range with a minimum and maximum price (often called a “fare corridor”, Tarifkorridor). Within the range, passenger and operator agree the price freely before the trip starts.

This lets taxis offer a fixed price for app bookings, similar to Mietwagen. Whether and how this applies to you is set in your local tariff.

2. Special agreements

With large customers, for example a company or a health insurer, the operator may agree a different price within the mandatory service area. But only if all four conditions are met § 51 (2) PBefG:

  1. There is a fixed period, a minimum number of trips or a minimum monthly turnover.
  2. The order of the transport market is not disturbed.
  3. Prices and conditions are agreed in writing.
  4. The regulation provides for approval or notification to the authority.

The mandatory service area

The mandatory service area (Pflichtfahrbereich) is the area where the taxi tariff applies, usually the municipality or the district. Only here do the tariff obligation and the obligation to carry apply § 47 (4) PBefG.

If a passenger travels beyond the mandatory service area, local rules decide how the price for the part outside is calculated. Often it may be agreed freely. Then agree it before the trip starts, otherwise there will be arguments. If nothing is agreed, the tariff usually continues to apply.

Aachen

According to the City of Aachen taxi tariff of 2021, the mandatory service area covers the whole StädteRegion Aachen. Always check the current version with the city or the StädteRegion.

The obligation to carry

A taxi must take passengers. This is the obligation to carry (Beförderungspflicht). It applies if all three conditions are met § 22 PBefG:

  1. The passenger complies with the conditions of carriage.
  2. The trip is possible with the vehicles normally used. (A normal taxi does not have to carry 8 people or a sofa.)
  3. The trip is not prevented by circumstances the operator cannot avoid. (For example: the road is closed due to a storm.)

For taxis it applies only in the mandatory service area. For Mietwagen it does not apply at all § 47 (4), § 49 (4) PBefG.

Not a reason to refuse
  • “The trip is too short.”
  • “I'd rather have a long trip.”
  • “My shift ends soon.” (as long as the taxi is on duty)
  • The passenger's origin, skin colour, religion or disability

When may the driver refuse or exclude someone?

SituationRule
Facts suggest the passenger is a danger to safety, the operation or other passengers, e.g. very drunk and aggressive.May refuse § 13 BOKraft
The passenger breaks the rules despite a warning, e.g. opens the door while driving or throws things out of the window.May exclude § 14 (4) BOKraft
The passenger wants to take dangerous substances: explosive, highly flammable, radioactive, corrosive or foul-smelling.These items are excluded § 15 BOKraft
Luggage and animalsThe passenger must stow them so they endanger nobody. Animals may not sit on the seats. § 15 BOKraft

Passengers must behave as safety and consideration for others require. They must follow the driver's instructions § 14 (1) BOKraft.

The obligation to operate

Anyone with a taxi licence must actually start the business and run it properly for the whole period of validity. This is the obligation to operate (Betriebspflicht). Only on application can the authority release you if operating is impossible or unreasonable § 21 PBefG. How many hours a taxi must be in service is often set in the local taxi regulations.

Prices for Mietwagen

  • The price is freely agreed. There is no tariff obligation and no obligation to carry.
  • If nothing else is agreed, the price is calculated from the reading of the odometer § 40 BOKraft.
  • The licensing authority may set minimum fares for its district to protect the taxi trade. Before that it consults the IHK, among others. If they exist, you must not charge less § 51a PBefG.
Remember

Taxi = tariff is mandatory (exceptions: fixed price/price range for pre-booking, special agreement). Mietwagen = free price, but possibly a minimum fare.

Typical mistakes

!“The IHK sets the taxi tariff.”The state government by regulation, usually delegated to the district or city. The IHK is only consulted.
!“I may give regular customers a discount.”Only if it applies to everyone under the same conditions, or as a special agreement under § 51 (2).
!“The obligation to carry applies everywhere.”Only in the mandatory service area, and only for taxis.
!“The roof sign must always be lit.”It is lit when the taxi is free. At the rank it need not be lit; with a passenger it is off.
!“A fixed price is allowed for every taxi trip.”Only for pre-booked trips and only if the local tariff provides for it.
