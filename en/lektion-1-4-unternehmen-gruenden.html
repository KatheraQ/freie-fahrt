Starting a business: trade and commercial law

Besides the licence under the PBefG, you must also register your business as a trade and choose a legal form. The legal form mainly decides whether you are liable with your private assets. Exam questions often ask about liability, minimum capital and the commercial register.

Exam area 1.2 / 1.7 · Trade law / Commercial lawapprox. 30 minutes§§ 14, 35 GewO§§ 1, 2, 17, 29, 105, 161 HGB§§ 5, 5a, 7 GmbHG§ 705 BGB§ 192 SGB VIIDeutsche Version

After this lesson you can

  • list the steps for founding a taxi or Mietwagen business
  • explain when an operator is a merchant (Kaufmann) and must be in the commercial register
  • compare sole proprietorship, GbR, OHG, KG, GmbH and UG
  • describe liability in each legal form
  • know the minimum capital and payment rules for GmbH and UG

Founding step by step

A taxi or Mietwagen business is a trade (Gewerbe). You therefore need two things: the licence under the PBefG and the trade registration (Gewerbeanzeige) under the Trade Regulation Act. A typical order:

  1. Pass the exam and prove your equity (lesson 1.2).
  2. Choose a legal form. For a GmbH or UG: articles of association at a notary, pay in capital, entry in the commercial register.
  3. Apply for the licence at the licensing authority.
  4. Register the trade at the municipal trade office (Gewerbeamt) § 14 GewO. The trade office informs the tax office, the IHK and the Berufsgenossenschaft, among others.
  5. Tax office: submit the tax registration questionnaire, receive a tax number.
  6. Berufsgenossenschaft: register the business with BG Verkehr within 1 week of starting § 192 SGB VII.
  7. With employees: apply for an employer number (Betriebsnummer) at the Federal Employment Agency and register staff with the health insurer (lesson 1.8).
  8. Vehicles: register, insure, have the taximeter or odometer calibrated (module 3).

With the trade registration you automatically become a member of the IHK.

Prohibition of trade

If a trader is unreliable, the authority can prohibit the trade fully or partly § 35 GewO. Unreliability is therefore doubly dangerous: for the licence and for the trade.

Am I a merchant (Kaufmann)?

The Commercial Code (Handelsgesetzbuch, HGB) applies to merchants. A merchant is anyone who runs a commercial business (Handelsgewerbe). Every trade is a commercial business unless, by its type or size, it does not need a business organised in a commercial way § 1 HGB.

Whether a commercial organisation is needed depends, for example, on turnover, number of employees and vehicles and the number of transactions. The HGB has no fixed threshold.

A GmbH or UG is always a merchant, however small. It is called a merchant by legal form (Formkaufmann).

Details that are often asked

  • GmbH: share capital (Stammkapital) at least 25.000 €. Before registration at least a quarter of each share must be paid in, in total at least half of the minimum capital, i.e. 12.500 € § 7 (2) GmbHG. The GmbH is run by the managing director (Geschäftsführer).
  • UG (haftungsbeschränkt): a “mini GmbH” with less than 25.000 € capital. The capital must be paid in fully in cash; contributions in kind (e.g. a car) are forbidden. Every year a quarter of the annual surplus must go into a reserve until 25.000 € are reached § 5a GmbHG.
  • GmbH & Co. KG: a KG whose general partner is a GmbH. In the end no natural person is liable without limit.
And the licence?

With a GmbH or UG, the company is the operator and receives the licence. Professional competence must be held by a person who manages the business, for example the managing director § 13 (1) No. 3 PBefG. A company only receives the licence document once it is entered in the register § 15 (2) PBefG.

Which legal form fits?

One vehicle, you drive yourselfUsually a sole proprietorship. Simple and cheap. But you are liable with everything you own.
Two partners, small businessGbR or, if the business is commercial, OHG. Both are fully liable.
Several vehicles, employees, limit the riskGmbH or, with little starting capital, UG. But: notary, commercial register, balance sheet. That costs time and money.
Limited liability has limits

Even with a GmbH you are personally liable if, for example, you sign a personal guarantee (Bürgschaft) for a loan. Banks often ask for this from small companies.

Typical mistakes

!“With the PBefG licence I don't need a trade registration.”You need both: the PBefG licence and the trade registration under § 14 GewO.
!“For a GmbH I must pay in 25.000 € immediately.”At least 12.500 € at registration (a quarter of each share, half in total).
!“For a UG I can contribute my car as capital.”Contributions in kind are forbidden for a UG. The capital must be fully paid in cash.
!“In a KG everyone is liable only up to their contribution.”Only the limited partner. The general partner is liable without limit.
!“In a GbR each partner is liable for half.”Each partner is liable without limit and personally for all debts.
